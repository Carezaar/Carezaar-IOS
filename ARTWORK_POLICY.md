# Artwork policy

Product artwork in Carezaar web and native iOS must not contain human photos, illustrations, cartoon people or faces. Android parity does not override this policy.

Allowed artwork includes hands-only images, faceless outline person icons, rooms, objects, homes, logos and plain icons. Keep the supplied hands-only Messages header and existing room replacements.

The owner clarified that this policy applies only to artwork supplied by the app. User-uploaded profile photos remain supported in matches, profiles and chats; do not hide or remove them under this artwork rule.

The owner's instruction on October 18, 2026 supersedes the image-restoration requests in A1, F1, F2, G1, M1 and M2. Keep the intentional removals from the "Update app artwork" commits. Do not restore the removed welcome photo, care-type pictures, people-based service/quality/shift artwork, rotating people headers or support illustration.

Use a new Profile illustration only if it meets this policy. Show uncertain images to the owner and wait for their decision before adding them.

Historical audit screenshots, source history and archived restoration evidence are not approved product artwork. Keep them out of product resources and shipped builds.
