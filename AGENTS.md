# Carezaar instructions

Read ARTWORK_POLICY.md before changing images, icons or illustrations. The owner's explicit people-free policy takes priority over Android parity screenshots and older audit requests.

Do not restore artwork removed by the “Update app artwork” commits. Do not add photos, illustrations or cartoons of people, or facial-feature assets. Hands-only imagery, simple faceless person outlines, rooms, objects, homes, logos and plain icons are allowed. Keep uncertain artwork out of the product until the owner has reviewed it.

Preserve the source-removal guards and resource-exclusion tests. Historical screenshots, archived artwork and Git history do not establish approval to use people imagery. Continue all independent non-art functional and accessibility work.

This rule covers built-in artwork only. The owner explicitly excluded user-uploaded photos; keep existing upload and display behavior in matches, profiles and chats.
